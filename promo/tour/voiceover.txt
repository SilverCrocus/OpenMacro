Meet OpenMacro: the free, open-source nutrition coach.

Instead of guessing your calories from a formula, it learns what you actually burn, and updates your targets every week.

Logging takes seconds. Search fifty-eight thousand foods, scan a barcode, or snap a photo and let AI fill your plate. Got something wrong? Just tell it.

Then watch your weight trend, energy balance and macros, without the daily noise.

No account. No subscription. No ads. Your data stays on your phone, and every line of code is open.

Get OpenMacro free on GitHub, and help us build it.
